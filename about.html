About Savanna Brew

More than coffee... it's a lifestyle!

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with a simple dream: to celebrate the rich taste of Kenyan coffee and create a welcoming place for everyone.

Our founder, Amina Otieno, grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri. Her love for coffee inspired her to share the flavours and traditions of Kenyan coffee with the community.

Today, we serve carefully prepared coffee and freshly baked treats while supporting local farmers and creating a cosy space for our customers.

What We Believe In

Our values guide everything we do.

Quality

We use freshly roasted Kenyan coffee beans to give our customers a memorable experience.

Community

We support local farmers and provide a friendly space where everyone feels welcome.

Sustainability

We encourage responsible packaging and reduce waste wherever possible.

"Every cup of coffee is an opportunity to bring people together and celebrate the beauty of Kenya."
