A Little About Us

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee.

Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today, the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month.

What Matters to Us

Our Values

Everything we do is guided by our love for good coffee, our community and the environment.

Quality

We use freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan beans to give every cup its distinctive flavour.

Community

We believe in fair prices for farmers and creating a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

We care for our environment through compostable cups and zero-waste pastries.

"Our dream is to celebrate the richness of Kenyan coffee while creating a warm and welcoming place where everyone feels at home."

— Amina Otieno, Founder of Savanna Brew Café
